## Project Overview

Market Basket Analysis for Retail Shopping is a data warehousing and data mining project designed to identify customer purchasing patterns and discover products frequently bought together.

The system processes retail transaction data through preprocessing and transformation, preparing it for efficient analysis. It applies data mining algorithms such as **Apriori** and **FP-Growth** to identify frequent itemsets and generate association rules using Support, Confidence and Lift.

The platform transforms raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights, helping retailers understand customer buying behaviour and make informed business decisions.

## Objectives

- Identify products frequently purchased together.
- Discover meaningful purchasing patterns from retail transaction data.
- Generate frequent itemsets using Apriori and FP-Growth.
- Generate association rules using Support, Confidence and Lift.
- Provide product bundle recommendations.
- Visualize product relationships.
- Generate AI-powered business insights.
- Support better retail decision-making through data-driven analysis.

## Key Features

### 1. Transaction Data Management
- Upload retail transaction datasets through CSV files.
- Validate and preprocess transaction data.
- Handle missing and invalid records.
- Transform transactions into basket format.

### 2. Frequent Itemset Mining
- Implement Apriori algorithm.
- Implement FP-Growth algorithm.
- Identify frequently purchased product combinations.
- Configure minimum support thresholds.

### 3. Association Rule Mining

Generate product association rules using:

- **Support:** Measures how frequently a product combination occurs in transactions.
- **Confidence:** Measures how often the consequent is purchased when the antecedent is purchased.
- **Lift:** Measures the strength of the relationship between products compared with independent purchasing.

### 4. Product Recommendations
- Generate recommendations based on selected products.
- Identify complementary products.
- Discover frequently purchased product bundles.

### 5. AI-Powered Business Advisor

Integrates Google Gemini to interpret analytical results and generate meaningful business insights.

The AI business advisor helps retailers understand purchasing relationships and identify potential business opportunities.

### 6. Reports and Data Export

Provides downloadable analytical reports in:

- CSV
- Excel
- PDF

### 7. Database Management

PostgreSQL integration is designed to provide persistent storage for uploaded datasets, transaction baskets, analysis sessions, frequent itemsets and association rules.

Historical analysis retrieval and dataset restoration are supported by the backend implementation.

## System Workflow

1. **Data Upload:** Retail transaction data is uploaded through the application.
2. **Preprocessing:** The dataset is validated, cleaned and transformed.
3. **Transaction Transformation:** Transactions are converted into basket format and encoded for analysis.
4. **Frequent Itemset Mining:** Apriori and FP-Growth identify frequently purchased product combinations.
5. **Association Rule Generation:** Rules are generated and evaluated using Support, Confidence and Lift.
6. **Recommendations:** The system discovers product relationships and generates basket recommendations and product bundles.
7. **AI Business Insights:** Gemini interprets analytical results and provides business-oriented insights.
8. **Visualization and Reporting:** Results are presented through the interface and can be exported as reports.
